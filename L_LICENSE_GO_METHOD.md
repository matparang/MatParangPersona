LEVEL 1: L LICENSE (GO METHOD) - AI Basics & Safety

L1.1: What is AI?

Core Concept: AI as problem-solving assistant, not magic
Key Principles:

· Pattern recognition from data
· Learning from examples
· Task automation
· Decision support

Malaysian Examples:

· Touch 'n Go fraud detection
· Bank fraud prevention systems
· eWallet security features

Teaching Approach (GO Method):

· Simple analogies: "AI like a very smart assistant"
· Focus on practical benefits
· Demystify AI as tool, not magic

Example Dialogue:
"AI macam kawan pandai yang boleh belajar dari pengalaman.Contoh di Malaysia, sistem Touch 'n Go guna AI untuk detect transaksi mencurigakan dan lindungi duit kita."

---

L1.2: Types of AI People Use Daily

Core Concept: AI is already in daily Malaysian life
Key Principles:

· Recommendation systems
· Voice assistants
· Image recognition
· Predictive analytics

Malaysian Examples:

· Shopee product recommendations
· Waze traffic predictions
· Google Assistant in BM/English
· Facebook friend suggestions

Teaching Approach:

· Show invisible AI in daily life
· Practical, recognizable examples
· Build AI awareness

Example Dialogue:
"Setiap hari kita guna AI tanpa sedar!Bila Shopee cadangkan produk anda mungkin suka, atau Waze bagi laluan elak traffic - itu semua AI bekerja."

---

L1.3: Difference Between AI and Google

Core Concept: AI creates, Google finds
Key Principles:

· Google: Information retrieval
· AI: Content generation
· Different purposes, different tools

Malaysian Examples:

· Google search vs ChatGPT
· Finding recipe vs AI creating new recipe
· Research vs brainstorming

Teaching Approach:

· Clear distinction with examples
· When to use each tool
· Complementary technologies

Example Dialogue:
"Google macam perpustakaan- cari maklumat yang sudah ada. AI macam chef - cipta kandungan baru dari pengetahuan sedia ada."

---

L1.4: AI Safety & Limitations

Core Concept: Understand what AI can and cannot do
Key Principles:

· AI can make mistakes
· Human verification needed
· Privacy considerations
· Ethical usage

Malaysian Examples:

· Verifying AI-generated information
· Protecting personal data
· Recognizing AI limitations in local context

Teaching Approach:

· Safety-first mindset
· Critical thinking development
· Responsible usage

Example Dialogue:
"AI boleh buat silap,jadi kita kena semak semua maklumat penting. Macam GPS kadang-kadang salah, kita masih guna common sense sendiri."
