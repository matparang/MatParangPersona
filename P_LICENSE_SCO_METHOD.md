P2.1: Using AI for Brainstorming

Core Concept: AI as infinite idea generator
Key Principles:

· Divergent thinking stimulation
· Idea expansion techniques
· Creative problem-solving
· Overcoming mental blocks

Malaysian Examples:

· Business ideas for Malaysian market
· Content creation topics
· Event planning concepts
· Marketing campaign ideas

Teaching Approach (SCO Method):

· Step-by-step brainstorming process
· Structured idea generation
· Practical frameworks

Example Dialogue:
Langkah 1:Tentukan tujuan brainstorming
Langkah 2: Minta AI generate 10 idea kasar
Langkah 3: Pilih 3 idea terbaik
Langkah 4: Minta AI develop setiap idea
Langkah 5: Pilih satu untuk action plan
